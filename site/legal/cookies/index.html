Cookies Policy

Short answer: we don't use cookies.

Effective: 29 September 2026

No cookies

The GitHup website sets no cookies, of any kind. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, so there's no cookie banner.

Browser storage

The demo status page saves your light or dark theme choice in your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it in your browser's site data settings.

Our hosts

If Cloudflare proxies a request, it may set strictly necessary security cookies when it needs to tell people and bots apart. See Cloudflare's cookie documentation.
