Opt-Out Preferences

The short version: there's nothing to opt out of.

Effective: 29 September 2026

No sale or sharing of data

Laws like California's CCPA/CPRA give you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data. The GitHup website collects no personal data for advertising or analytics, so there is nothing to sell, share or opt out of.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Because nothing here tracks you, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals have no additional effect. We honour them anyway.

Questions

If you have a question about data handling that isn't answered here, contact legal@stux.group.
