Privacy Policy

What GitHup's website collects: next to nothing.

Effective: 29 September 2026

The short version

The GitHup website is a static site. It has no accounts, no forms, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. We don't collect personal data about you.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its domain is managed by Cloudflare, which may also proxy requests to it. Like any web host, they may process your IP address, browser details and the pages you request in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. That processing is covered by GitHub's privacy statement and Cloudflare's privacy policy. We don't receive or keep those logs.

Live status data

The home page and the demo load status data from this site and from raw.githubusercontent.com (GitHub). Only the site's own monitoring data is fetched; nothing about you is sent.

Images from Stux.Group

The Stux.Group logo is loaded from global.media.stux.group. Those requests carry no cookies and are not used to track you.

Using the GitHup action

GitHup itself runs in your GitHub repository, under your account. The monitoring data, Issues and status page it creates live in your repo; Stux.Group never receives them.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask what personal data we hold about you and have it corrected or deleted. For this site the answer is that we hold none, but you're welcome to ask at legal@stux.group.
